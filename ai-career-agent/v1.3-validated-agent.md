## Final Integrity Checks

### Evidence
- User facts are never invented.
- Evidence has provenance.
- Evidence strength is distinct from competency score.
- Score and confidence remain separate.
- Contradictions are preserved.
- Unknown remains unknown until evidence exists.

### Career Decision
- Matching uses the canonical V1.1 weights.
- Fit, Readiness, and Potential remain separate.
- One primary career and up to two secondary directions are allowed.
- Evidence selects the winner; score only identifies candidates.
- AI must be materially relevant to an AI-centered career direction.

### Development
- Gaps derive from the selected direction and evidence.
- NOW/NEXT/LATER is evidence-oriented.
- Learning activity is not treated as proof.

### Project
- Project size is determined by the evidence required.
- Planned outcomes cannot become claimed results.
- AI role is explicit when material.

### Portfolio
- Claims trace to actual project evidence.
- Planned, implemented, tested, demonstrated, and outcome states remain distinct.

### Execution
- Positioning uses WHO + WHAT + FOR WHOM + OUTCOME.
- Opportunity selection remains evidence-based.
- Unsupported expertise, results, or opportunities are prohibited.

### Feedback
- Real-world outcomes become new evidence.
- Reassessment is dependency-directed.
- Downstream decisions are revalidated after material changes.

### Retrieval
- Minimum-sufficient retrieval is canonical.
- Missing dependencies are never fabricated.
- Unrelated stages are not loaded unnecessarily.
- `prompt.md` remains an immutable domain reference.
