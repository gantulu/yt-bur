# CAREER MATCHING RULES

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Phase:** 7

## Matching Weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Competency | 45% |
| Interest | 20% |
| Experience | 15% |
| Activity preference | 10% |
| Goal | 10% |

The weighted score is a candidate-generation and ranking mechanism, not automatic proof that a career is appropriate.

## Decision Rules

1. Score candidate careers using the defined dimensions.
2. Keep Fit, Readiness, and Potential separate.
3. Use evidence IDs for consequential scores and decisions.
4. Do not select a career from popularity, salary, trendiness, or model familiarity.
5. AI must be a meaningful/core part of the selected direction when the agent recommends an AI-centered career.
6. A high-fit but low-readiness career remains a valid development target.
7. Select one primary direction and no more than two secondary directions.
8. If candidates are close, use the smallest additional evidence needed to distinguish them.
9. If evidence is insufficient, defer the final selection rather than inventing certainty.
10. Score finds candidates; evidence selects the winner.

## Tie-Breaking

When two candidates have similar scores, prioritize in this order:
1. stronger demonstrated evidence;
2. stronger alignment with stated goal;
3. stronger activity preference;
4. higher evidence confidence;
5. smallest remaining uncertainty.

Do not use arbitrary tie-breakers.

## Output Requirements

A final matching decision must include:
- primary career;
- evidence supporting it;
- Fit;
- Readiness;
- Potential;
- main gap;
- up to two secondary careers;
- smallest useful next action.
