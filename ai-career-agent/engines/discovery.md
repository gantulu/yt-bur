# DISCOVERY ENGINE

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Phase:** 5

## Purpose
Progressively discover the user's strongest AI-centered career direction by maximizing useful evidence while minimizing unnecessary questions.

## Modes
### Explore
Use when little evidence exists. Broadly identify interests, preferences, goals, work style, and meaningful activities.

### Validate
Use when a career direction is emerging. Test whether the direction is supported by stronger evidence such as experience, concrete examples, projects, or results.

### Resolve
Use when evidence conflicts or two directions remain difficult to distinguish. Ask the smallest question that can resolve the material uncertainty.

## Operating Loop
1. Inspect current state.
2. Identify the highest-impact uncertainty.
3. Select one primary question.
4. Present numbered choices when useful.
5. Allow an "other" response.
6. Extract evidence from the answer.
7. Classify and record the evidence.
8. Update affected state.
9. Reassess uncertainty.
10. Continue, transition to assessment, or stop when sufficient.

## Question Selection
Priority is determined by:
competency gap + uncertainty + career relevance + evidence value − repetition penalty

Prefer questions that:
- distinguish multiple career directions;
- reveal behavior rather than abstract preference;
- convert self-report into concrete examples;
- test AI-centered motivation;
- identify constraints that materially affect feasibility.

Avoid:
- generic personality quizzes;
- questions with no downstream decision value;
- repeating known information;
- premature career recommendations.

## Evidence Escalation
When possible, move from weaker to stronger evidence:
Interest → Preference → Self-report → Experience → Concrete example → Project → Demonstrated result

Do not force stronger evidence when it does not exist.

## Discovery Limits
- Minimum: 8 meaningful evidence units.
- Target: 10–12.
- Maximum: 15 questions/evidence-gathering turns unless a higher-authority rule changes the limit.
- Stop early when the strongest direction is sufficiently stable and additional questions are unlikely to change the decision.

## Transition
Discovery may transition to assessment when:
- enough evidence exists;
- major uncertainties are resolved or explicitly bounded;
- evidence spans the dimensions needed for career matching;
- no critical contradiction remains unresolved.

If not ready, ask one focused question rather than recommending a career.
