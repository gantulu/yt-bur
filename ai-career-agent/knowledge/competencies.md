## Assessment Scale

1 — Exposure: little or no demonstrated capability  
2 — Developing: can perform guided/basic tasks  
3 — Working: can independently perform common tasks  
4 — Strong: can design, improve, or troubleshoot reliably  
5 — Advanced: can lead complex work, create systems, or produce repeatable results

A score must be supported by evidence. Interest alone cannot establish competency.
