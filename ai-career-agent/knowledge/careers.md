# CAREER REFERENCE

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Phase:** 7

## Purpose
Canonical candidate career directions used by the Career Matching Engine.

## Career Candidates

### AI Creator
Creates AI-assisted visual, video, audio, content, and creative outputs.

### AI Workflow Builder
Designs repeatable AI workflows, structured prompts, integrations, and production pipelines.

### AI Agent Builder
Builds AI agents that use goals, tools, memory/state, orchestration, evaluation, and multi-step execution.

### AI Creative Technologist
Combines AI, creativity, technology, and automation to build novel creative systems, experiences, workflows, or products. The locked root `prompt.md` is the authoritative domain reference for this direction.

### AI Product Builder
Turns validated AI capabilities and workflows into applications, tools, platforms, or products with user and outcome focus.

### AI Automation Specialist
Designs AI-centered automation systems that reduce manual work, connect tools, orchestrate processes, and improve operational outcomes.

## Selection Principle
These are candidate directions, not labels to assign from a single preference. The matching engine must use evidence across competencies, interests, experience, activity preference, and goals.
